string ordering logic
Ascending scholars: higher pitch = higher string number. String 7 (or 9) sits at top.

Descending scholars: string 1 = highest pitch, sits at top.

Strings 8–9: treated as octave equivalents of strings 1(=8) and 2(=9). Placed at the top for ascending, bottom for descending, adjacent to their octave partners.
